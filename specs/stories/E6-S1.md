# E6-S1: Statement generation and matching engine

- **Epic:** E6 - Reconciliation
- **Layer:** Service
- **Group:** E
- **Depends on:** E2-S4

## Description

Generates rail statements from the mock adapters and matches them against payments on payment id, rail reference and exact paise amount. Targets success metric M4.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Generating a statement for a date persists one RailStatementEntry per COMPLETED payment with rail, payment_ref, amount_paise and statement_date (feature F091)
2. An entry matching payment id, rail reference and amount is recorded as matched (feature F092)
3. An entry whose amount differs by 1 paise is recorded as amount_mismatch (feature F093)
4. A COMPLETED payment with no entry is unmatched_internal, and an entry with no payment is unmatched_external (feature F094)
5. Against the seeded corpus in specs/test_artefacts, 100% of seeded anomalies are flagged and no clean entry is flagged (feature F095)
6. Re-running reconciliation for the same date creates no duplicate result rows (feature F096)
